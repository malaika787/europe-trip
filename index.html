Good to know

  • UK ETA. Everyone needs their own approved ETA before boarding in Boston. It's linked to your passport, so there's nothing to print.
  • Paying for transit in London. Tap the same contactless card or phone every time so the daily price cap kicks in. Each person needs their own.
  • Eurostar. Get to St Pancras about 90 minutes early. Security and French passport control both happen before you board.
  • Tickets. Abbey tickets are printed. Orsay and Louvre are in your phone wallet. Keep screenshots as a backup.
  • Taxis save legs. In Paris, a taxi between far-apart stops costs little split four ways and keeps energy up for the museums.
  • Sundays. The Boqueria market is closed Sunday, so see it Saturday evening.
Times are local. Check opening hours on each official site the day before.
